Help

Getting started

What is Hire From SA?

A job board where businesses hire vetted virtual assistants from South Africa. You apply to jobs, chat with hirers and get hired, all in one place.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free for VAs to apply, get hired and get paid.

Why did I have to verify my identity?

It keeps hirers and VAs safe. Once our team approves you, your profile shows "Vetted by our team", which helps hirers trust you.

Applying for jobs

How do I apply for a job?

Open Apply for Jobs, pick a job and press Apply now. Introduce yourself in one sentence, answer the hirer's questions and add your bid.

What is a bid?

Your bid is the rate you'd like for the job, plus the range you'd accept. The hirer sees it next to your application. The rate you agree on is the rate you're paid.

How do I stand out?

Complete your profile: add a clear photo, record your one-minute intro video and set your rate and hours. Then keep your one-sentence introduction specific to the job.

Messages and hiring

How will I know if a hirer replies?

Replies show in Messages and at the top of Applications under "Needs your reply". Reply quickly, hirers often talk to several VAs at once.

Can a hirer message me if I did not apply?

Yes. Hirers who find your profile can message you directly. Those conversations show in Messages as "Direct message from a hirer".

What do Pending, Hired and Not selected mean?

Pending means the hirer hasn't decided yet. Hired means you got the job. Not selected means the hirer went with someone else, so keep applying.

Should I share my email or WhatsApp?

Keep conversations on Hire From SA. Your contact details stay private, and it protects you if anything goes wrong with a hirer.

Payments

How do I get paid?

Once you are hired, we email you to add your payout method. Your earnings and payment history then show on the Payments page.

Are there any fees for me?

No. You keep 100% of the rate you agree with the hirer.

Profile and account

How do I update my profile, photo or resume?

Open My Profile from the menu under your photo. Press Edit profile, tap the camera on your photo, or use Replace resume.

How do I change my name or password?

Open Settings from the menu under your photo. To change your email, email support from your current address.

How do I close my account?

Open Settings and press Request account closure at the bottom.
