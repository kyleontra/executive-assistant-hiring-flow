QUESTIONS

Good to know before you apply.

Is it really free?

Yes. You never pay to sign up, apply or get hired. We make our money from the client's side, never from you.

What do I need to sign up?

Your resume (a file or a Google Docs, Drive or Dropbox link), a South African ID, and a phone or computer with a camera for a short ID video.

How do I get paid?

Clients pay through the platform, and we send your money straight to your bank account within 24 hours of receiving it.

How long does approval take?

Our team reviews every sign-up, usually within 24 hours. You can browse jobs while you wait, and you can apply once you're approved.

Is there anything I have to agree to?

One rule: when you find a client through Hire From SA, you keep working with them and getting paid through the platform. That's what keeps it free for you.
